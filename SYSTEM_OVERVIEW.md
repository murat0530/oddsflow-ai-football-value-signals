# OddsFlow v8 — System Overview

## The Signal Pipeline — From Players to Signals

OddsFlow's core advantage is that signal generation **does not begin at kickoff**. By the time a match starts, the engine has already modeled every plausible outcome. Live signals are generated by comparing real-time events against a pre-built probability foundation.

### OddsFlow Score (Player-Level Rating)

Every player across all monitored leagues is assigned a proprietary **OddsFlow Score** — a composite metric that quantifies a player's expected contribution to match dynamics.

- **Data sources:** Publicly available statistics combined with institutional-grade professional data (scouting databases, performance tracking systems).
- **Scope:** All players in every monitored match — not just starters, but substitutes who may impact the game.
- **Parameters:** Not publicly disclosed. The OddsFlow Score is a proprietary formula.
- **Purpose:** Serves as the foundational input for all probability modeling. Two matches with identical team names but different starting XIs will produce entirely different simulation outputs.

### 3-Phase Execution Model

```
Phase 1: PRE-LINEUP — AI Analysis
    │  OddsFlow Scores + historical matchup data + league context
    │  → Baseline probability model (expected squad compositions)
    ↓
Phase 2: POST-LINEUP — 10,000 Monte Carlo Simulations
    │  Confirmed lineups feed into the simulation engine
    │  → 10,000 match scenarios computed: scorelines, goal timing,
    │     momentum patterns, substitution impacts, all possibilities mapped
    │  → Complete probability foundation established BEFORE kickoff
    ↓
Phase 3: LIVE MATCH — Real-Time Edge Detection
    │  Live match state compared against the 10,000-run foundation
    │  → When real-time events create divergence from market pricing,
    │     and the foundation confirms the edge, a signal is generated
    ↓
Signal Output → Strategy Systems (S1–S4.3) → Risk Management → Execution
```

**Why this matters:** Most betting models react to what's happening. OddsFlow already knows what *could* happen — all 10,000 versions of it. The system doesn't predict the future; it recognizes when the present is mispriced relative to a pre-computed probability map.

## System Architecture

OddsFlow processes live match data through a **5-phase operational pipeline**:

```
Phase 1: Data Ingestion
    ↓  Live odds, scores, statistics from multiple sources
Phase 2: Processing & Analysis
    ↓  Market pressure calculation, momentum detection, statistical modeling
Phase 3: Signal Generation
    ↓  Strategy-specific signal evaluation across S1–S4.3
Phase 4: Risk Management
    ↓  Multi-layer filtering, position control, anomaly detection
Phase 5: Execution & Reporting
    ↓  Bet placement, live tracking, automated settlement & reporting
```

Each phase operates independently, allowing signals to be generated, validated, and executed within seconds of market movement.

## Strategy Systems

### S1 — Conservative

**Role**: High-confidence, low-frequency signals on premium matches.

S1 applies the strictest entry criteria of all pressure-based systems. It focuses on strong favorites in major leagues during the later stages of matches, when market signals are most reliable.

- **Markets**: HDP + OU + 1X2
- **Style**: Wait for clear confirmation before entry
- **Frequency**: Low — only fires when conditions are highly favorable
- **Strength**: Capital preservation, minimal drawdown

---

### S2 — Active Trader

**Role**: Early-entry momentum strategy capturing opening pressure shifts.

S2 identifies matches where home advantage and early market pressure create favorable entry points. It enters earlier than other systems, capturing value before the market adjusts.

- **Markets**: HDP + OU
- **Style**: Aggressive early positioning
- **Frequency**: High — most active of the pressure systems
- **Strength**: Volume generation, early value capture

---

### S3 — Sniper

**Role**: Selective targeting of specific match profiles.

S3 combines pressure analysis with match context to identify opportunities in both favorite and underdog scenarios. It balances frequency and precision, acting as the middle ground between S1's conservatism and S2's aggression.

- **Markets**: HDP + OU
- **Style**: Patient, selective entries
- **Frequency**: Medium
- **Strength**: Balanced risk-reward across diverse match types

---

### S4.1 — MC Only (Pure AI)

**Role**: Data-driven signal generation using Monte Carlo simulation.

S4.1 relies entirely on AI-powered match simulation to estimate outcome probabilities. It compares model-derived probabilities against market-implied probabilities to identify edges, without requiring pressure-based confirmation.

- **Markets**: HDP + OU + 1X2
- **Style**: Pure quantitative, model-driven
- **Frequency**: High — generates signals whenever the model detects value
- **Strength**: Captures edges invisible to pressure-based analysis

---

### S4.2 — Hybrid (AI + Pressure) ⭐

**Role**: Dual-confirmation system combining AI modeling with market pressure validation.

S4.2 is the flagship system. It requires **both** the Monte Carlo model to identify an edge **and** market pressure signals to confirm the direction. This two-gate approach dramatically reduces false positives.

- **Markets**: HDP + OU + 1X2
- **Style**: High-conviction, dual-validated
- **Frequency**: Lower than S4.1/S4.3 (stricter criteria)
- **Strength**: Highest hit rate and ROI across all systems

**Why it works**: AI models can identify mathematical edges, but not all edges materialize. Market pressure — the aggregate behavior of professional bettors — acts as a real-world confirmation layer. When both signals align, the probability of a successful outcome increases significantly.

---

### S4.3 — Blend (AI-Pressure Fusion)

**Role**: Weighted probability fusion of AI and pressure models.

S4.3 takes a different approach from S4.2's binary confirmation. Instead of requiring both systems to agree, it creates a **blended probability** by weighting AI model output and pressure-derived probabilities. This produces a smoother, more continuous signal distribution.

- **Markets**: HDP + OU + 1X2
- **Style**: Probabilistic fusion
- **Frequency**: High — large sample size
- **Strength**: Stable output, diversified signal source

## Smart Money Detection

OddsFlow includes a dedicated smart money detection module that monitors for professional betting activity across the market. It tracks four distinct signal types:

| Signal Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Steam Move** | Sudden, sharp odds movement driven by high-volume professional action |
| **Cross-Book Arbitrage** | Price discrepancies between bookmakers indicating informed money |
| **Reverse Line Movement (RLM)** | Odds moving opposite to public betting percentages |
| **Closing Line Value (CLV)** | Tracking whether entries beat the closing line — the gold standard of betting skill |

Smart money signals achieve a **63.5% hit rate** and are used as an additional confirmation layer across all strategy systems.

## Risk Management

OddsFlow employs a multi-layer risk management framework:

- **Opponent Pressure Analysis** — Monitors counter-signals that may invalidate an entry
- **Time-Based Controls** — Entry windows and lockout periods to avoid volatile match phases
- **Position Sizing** — Dynamic stake calculation based on edge magnitude and confidence
- **Per-Match Exposure Limits** — Maximum capital allocation per fixture
- **Anomaly Detection** — Identifies and filters unusual market behavior (e.g., match-fixing indicators)
- **Drawdown Protection** — Session-level and system-level loss limits

The risk layer operates independently of signal generation, ensuring that even high-conviction signals are subject to portfolio-level constraints.
